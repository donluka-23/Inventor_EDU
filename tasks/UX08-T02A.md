5. Frozen validation behavior and errors
Validate strictly and fail closed before producing a projection:
- PROJECTION_INVALID_INPUT: any argument is not a native valid instance of the specified input type, or fails strict Pydantic revalidation.
- PROJECTION_SNAPSHOT_MISMATCH: topology.snapshot_sha256 != report.snapshot_digest.
- PROJECTION_TERMINAL_NOT_IN_TOPOLOGY: the selected canonical terminal key is absent from topology.terminal_to_net.
- PROJECTION_INCONSISTENT_TOPOLOGY: the selected terminal's net is missing from topology.nets or its members do not include the canonical terminal key, or terminal-to-net/net-members consistency otherwise fails for a record under inspection.
- PROJECTION_INCONSISTENT_NATIVE_RECORD: any report record (finding or coverage) has a different snapshot_digest, catalog_digest, factset_digest or ruleset_version from the report, or its affected_net is inconsistent with the topology for its canonical terminal; missing canonical terminal in the topology is also inconsistent.
- PROJECTION_DUPLICATE_RECORD_ID: a finding_id repeats anywhere across report.findings and report.coverage.
No silently dropped invalid records. Do not fabricate a generic SOURCE_VERIFIED, CURRENT, STALE, SAFE, 5V_SAFE, global health grade, source URL, citation, voltage judgment or check status. This task does not prove that a passed-in native report is authentic/trusted, nor that its source pins remain current. It only rejects internal inconsistencies detectable from these inputs. topology does not carry a catalog digest, so do not claim to verify the topology's independent catalog trust.

ProjectionInputError.code is the stable machine-readable indicator. No fallback to guessed data, silent enum normalization or correction. The error message may include only non-sensitive identifiers; do not include confidential content or complete source payloads.

1. User-visible, falsifiable mission
Implement one working headless educational engineering cycle driven entirely by actual outputs from existing CAP-001 → SYN-001 → CORE-001A/B:
1. Discover reviewed physical GPIO contacts for a typed learning goal.
2. Generate a real Trig/Echo connection candidate; evaluate it using the existing CORE and GPIO verifier.
3. When sensor/Trig → board/J2.5 yields FAIL / OUTPUT_NOT_CAPABLE, generate a component- and terminal-specific Georgian causal hint with a verifiable source/claim trail and a bounded next action.
4. After the learner elects a different candidate (the fixture-supported board/J3.9), recompute a new canonical snapshot and verifier result; do not fabricate a correction from prose, labels, or old findings.
5. Withdraw the obsolete failure hint, generate a new scoped confirmation, and continue to disclose BOARD_AVAILABILITY=UNKNOWN, ACTIVE_CONFIGURATION=UNKNOWN, and ELECTRICAL_COMPATIBILITY=NOT_EVALUATED.
6. Demonstrate the whole cycle from one reproducible CLI command, with a readable Georgian learning transcript and machine-readable evidence/trace output.
Educational value gate: The result must explain what condition failed, why the rule applies to the exact physical terminal, what the learner can check or change, and which claims remain unverified. A formatter that merely translates OUTPUT_NOT_CAPABLE into a Georgian phrase does not pass.

This is a test-only, bounded tutoring capability. It is not a validated intelligent tutor, an electrical simulator, a real-hardware safety certification, a production source-trust system, or proof of educational effectiveness.

The owner selects for EDU-001:
- Constraint-based tutoring / model-tracing inspiration: Feedback is tied to a demonstrated rule, exact learner action, and computed state. No general claim of a full cognitive tutor.
- Formative feedback: Explanation of the specific mistake + one actionable next step + scope of uncertainty.
- Cognitive-load restraint: One prioritized primary hint for the chosen anchor, with remaining issues separately accessible. This is a design hypothesis, not proven learning superiority.
- Deterministic H1 template rendering: Human-reviewed Georgian wording and versioned reason/rule mappings; no LLM.
- Evidence provenance: Source claim → authoritative verifier finding/coverage → physical subject → educational claim. A digest or self-supplied trusted pin does not establish external manufacturer authenticity.
These choices are based on Research H's treatment of Anderson et al. (1995), Mitrovic/Koedinger/Martin (2003), Shute (2008), Hattie & Timperley (2007), cognitive-load theory, and W3C PROV-O. They justify the design and test approach, not measured effectiveness of INVENTOR EDU.

3. Approved scope of software
Create a pure read-only educational adapter plus a small, explicit tutoring session transition function. An illustrative public surface is:

build_educational_feedback(candidate, catalog, fact_set, learning_context, *, current_pins, optional_contextual_checks) -> EducationalFeedbackReport

and

transition_feedback(previous_feedback, new_candidate, ...) -> FeedbackTransition

These are illustrative names, not a mandate to duplicate existing types. Choose the fewest necessary immutable DTOs and document actual exact signatures. The adapter must not modify CORE, B, CAP, SYN, or their findings.

Minimal output responsibilities:
- EducationalHint: exact instance_id/`terminal_id`, optional real net_id; originating requirement ID, reason_code, rule and status; hint category; learning concept key; reviewed Georgian template key/version; rendered text; explicitly qualified next action; actual finding/coverage refs; source/evidence bindings; unresolved scopes; dependency fingerprint; validity.
- EducationalFeedbackReport: exact candidate snapshot_digest, catalog/factset/ruleset and template versions, current active hints, suppressed or stale hints, overall TEST_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION restriction.
- FeedbackTransition: old snapshot → new snapshot, withdrawn previous hint IDs, newly generated current hint IDs, no re-use of obsolete PASS/FAIL IDs as current.
The report's current statuses must come from actual raw verifier records. A separate educational VALID/STALE/WITHDRAWN is not an engineering PASS/FAIL.
Supported educational categories
Implement only categories that can be grounded in existing evidence:
1. ERROR_CAUSE — proven FAIL and exact reason.
2. SCOPED_CONFIRMATION — exact PASS and its rule, together with remaining unknown/unevaluated checks.
3. MISSING_EVIDENCE — UNKNOWN with source/mode prerequisites, no inferred numeric values.
4. NOT_EVALUATED_EXPLANATION — an actual GpioCoverage, not a fake finding.
5. CONTEXTUAL_CAUTION — only where an independent existing B rule is explicitly invoked and has a real result.
6. NEXT_ACTION_CANDIDATE — an action to test, never an automatically safe repair.
Do not create component facts, prices, voltages, resistor values, firmware behavior, or source locators out of a language template.
Source qualification
Build an exact, read-only claim resolver for the claims actually referenced by the chosen finding:
- Existing CatalogSnapshot.claims and source_documents;
- existing GpioFactSet.mappings, .silicon, .resources, .straps, and .sources;
- catalog/factset scope and version pins;
- recorded locator/review state.
A factual sentence about a specific pin/function needs the corresponding actual reviewed claim and source locator, plus the authoritative finding. A computed net-membership sentence instead needs CORE topology/snapshot evidence, not an invented manufacturer claim. Preserve these source classes distinctly.

If a required claim cannot be resolved or is conflicted, unreviewed, stale, wrong-scope, or absent, suppress the unsupported factual fragment, emit a neutral EVIDENCE_REQUEST/limitation, and record why. Do not use fallback guesses. Avoid copying manufacturer PDF prose or images.

Use only approved ka-GE templates and exact placeholders validated against allowed values. GPIO34, J2.5, Trig, Echo, claim IDs and units must remain technically accurate. For PASS, wording must say which check passed, never "the circuit is correct/safe".
Repair semantics
VERIFIED_CORRECTION is allowed only with an explicit named scope and a fresh real verifier PASS for every predicate claimed fixed; it must never suggest global circuit correctness. Prefer CANDIDATE_ACTION for a learner's proposed next connection. Whenever unsupported electrical or firmware scope remains, disclose that.

6. No architectural quality regressions
Hard invariants (failure of any means BLOCKED, even if all other tests pass):
- Never infer engineering truth from reason_code alone; require matching real finding/coverage plus source and exact scope.
- UNKNOWN, NOT_EVALUATED, NOT_APPLICABLE, FAIL, and scoped PASS must remain distinct.
- GpioCoverage.finding_id must be represented as a coverage reference, not a manufactured evaluated finding.
- Exact physical anchors must be taken from TerminalRef and live topology, never labels, pixel positions or guesses.
- Reject/caveat any finding whose snapshot_digest, catalog_digest or factset_digest does not match the current candidate and admitted context.
- If requirements digest, source/claim revision, ruleset or Georgian template version changes, invalidate or re-evaluate affected hint dependencies; when dependency completeness cannot be proven, fail closed.
- No confident numeric voltage or resistor recommendation without approved source-backed computation.
- No mutation to CORE reports and no second GPIO/electrical verifier.
- No claim of full ESP32 coverage or independently demonstrated educational effectiveness.
- No remote model calls, online dependency, learner tracking, analytics, telemetry or PII collection.
- TEST_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION remains visible in every report and demo output.
Fail-closed when source data is missing
It is acceptable for the tutoring engine to produce a precise EVIDENCE_MISSING or UNSUPPORTED_SCOPE explanation rather than a rich hint. It is not acceptable to invent a pedagogically polished but technically unsupported hardware claim.

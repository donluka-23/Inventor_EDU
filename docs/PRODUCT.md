# Product definition — INVENTOR EDU

**Status: ACCEPTED direction / market assumptions NOT YET VALIDATED**

## Positioning
A Georgian-first educational engineering workspace that helps beginners connect supported embedded-electronics components and understand objective electrical/pin-compatibility findings in a visual 2D interface.

## Initial audience
- Learner: Georgian-speaking school STEM learner or beginner technical student.
- Facilitator: teacher/mentor guiding an exercise.
- Buyer: **unknown** (schools, learning centres, teachers, or learners). Do not conflate user with buyer.

## First user job
"I want to connect one supported controller board to a supported sensor, see which pins may be used, and understand why a particular connection is rejected or cannot yet be checked."

## Value hypothesis (UNVERIFIED)
Compared with ordinary wiring instructions or existing tools, Georgian explanations integrated with precise diagram warnings may reduce critical wiring mistakes and teacher intervention, while improving understanding. No superiority or willingness to pay has been demonstrated.

## Differentiation candidates, not claims
- Georgian explanations and local teaching vocabulary.
- Beginner-centric and accessible 2D wiring UX.
- Evidence-linked correction rather than plausible but unverified chat advice.
- Teacher-ready exercises and feedback (after first technical gate).

## Explicit non-goals for first release
Universal invention assistant; unrestricted AI circuit generation; full PCB/EDA design; generic electronics physics engine; full ESP32 firmware emulation; all-board catalog; auto-correcting unsupported designs; marketplace; 3D visualisation.

## Strategic guardrails
First show one real feature working end-to-end before adding modules. Scope is a PRODUCT with a validated teaching and verification job, not a collection of computational libraries. Market adoption is a separate evidence gate from technical function.

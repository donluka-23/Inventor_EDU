# Pre-acceleration demo acceptance (working contract)

## Claim allowed ONLY if achieved
For one **exactly identified** board/sensor revision pair, INVENTOR EDU renders a user-editable 2D circuit and automatically identifies a small set of experimentally verified wiring incompatibilities with Georgian explanations and source-backed next steps.

## Non-negotiable demo checks
1. Learner can connect components and move them without changing electrical semantics.
2. At least one real invalid pin-function case triggers a finding from the engine, not hardcoded onClick UI.
3. At least one real electrical voltage-limit case triggers a finding, with authoritative part-specific facts.
4. Fixing a connection triggers recomputation and removes the corresponding warning.
5. An unknown variant or missing model produces visible UNKNOWN and no fabricated correctness claim.
6. Findings can be traced to exact pin/net, rule, data source and model assumptions.
7. No functionality beyond implemented support is described as simulated or validated.

## Evidence bundle
- Reproducible input fixture and expected output.
- Actual CLI/test output and version/commit.
- At least one independent check or physical measurement where safe and feasible.
- Screen capture of working, non-mocked interactions.
- Clearly disclosed unsupported cases.

## Commercial hypothesis separate
Do not claim product-market fit or adoption just because a technical demo works. User testing of Georgian UX and school purchasing feasibility are additional milestones.
